# Colour palettes and what they mean

Each palette in the generator (`src/lib/palettes.ts`) names five colours by role: outline, band, centre
line, ground and accent. The knot and the scrolls use outline, band, line and ground; the alkhan border
uses band, accent and ground. This page gives the meaning behind each palette and how well that meaning
is sourced.

Evidence labels: [S] source read; [S-sum] seen only as an abstract, snippet or summary; [B] blog, social
or trade page. **The hex values are approximations chosen for contrast, not colours taken from any
source.** Band/ground and line/band contrast is at least 3.3 : 1 (WCAG formula) in every palette.

## 1. Palettes

| Palette | Combination documented? | Meaning documented? |

| State flag · Төрийн далбаа | Yes [S] | Yes: the Law on State Symbols (1994), arts. 4 and 11, defines red as the symbol of flourishing (мандан бадрах), blue as the Eternal Sky (мөнх хөх тэнгэр), with the gold Soyombo [S] |

| Five-colour khadag · Таван өнгийн хадаг | The five colours and their order (цэнхэр, шар, цагаан, улаан, ногоон) [S-sum] | Lasting merit, prosperity and peace: galuut.com, mnb.mn [S-sum]. Which colour takes which role is this project's choice. |
| Wind-horse flag · Хийморийн дарцаг | The five flag colours [S] | Blue sky, white wind, red fire, green water, yellow earth: Wikipedia, *Prayer flag*, citing a flag seller [S]. A Tibetan scheme; the five Buddha families map the elements differently (water blue, wind green). |
| Ger pillars · Тооно, багана | Dark-red woodwork, blue-green heads edged in gold: a blog on Mongolian architecture colour [B] and a presentation on ger interiors [S-sum] | Weak: blog-grade sources only |

Only the flag and the emblem have both their colours and their meaning from a primary source.

## 2. Where sources disagree

- **Red.** The flag law makes red positive (flourishing). A study of colour words in the *Secret History of
  the Mongols* (Badamdorj 2023) groups red with black and brown among negative colours [S-sum].
- **Black.** Sources give it as negative (war, darkness, treachery), as "unmixed", as power and order, and
  as one of the two founding colours with white. No source found prescribes black for outlines; outlining
  in dark ink is a painting practice seen on objects, not a documented rule.
- **Elements.** The prayer-flag scheme (green water, white wind) and the five-Buddha-family scheme (blue
  water, green wind) disagree.

## 3. Not found

- S. Dulam, *Монгол бэлгэдэл зүй: Өнгөний бэлгэдэл зүй* (2000/2011), the standard Mongolian reference on
  colour symbolism, is not online and was not read.
- No source on the colour combinations of felt (ширдэг) or deel trim.
- Stepped shading of ugalz bands ("солонгоруулан будах", "дагнаслан будах") is named only by a blog and a
  search summary; no source gives the number or order of steps.
